# Bundled sound packs

The packs in this folder are made by the generator in
`src-tauri/examples/make_packs/`: synthesised from code, and for some
packs layered over single key presses cut from recordings that are
dedicated to the public domain (CC0). No other audio is used.

To the extent possible under law, the Clatterkey contributors
have waived all copyright and related or neighbouring rights to these sound
files, under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain
dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## What each pack is made from

- `blue` (Blue switches): synthesised from code.
- `brown` (Brown switches): synthesised from code.
- `red` (Red switches): synthesised from code.
- `typewriter` (Typewriter): synthesised from code.
- `thocky-linear` (Thocky linear): synthesised from code.
- `fast-linear` (Fast linear): synthesised from code.
- `sharp-clicky` (Sharp clicky): synthesised from code.
- `tactile-bump` (Tactile bump): synthesised from code.

To regenerate them after changing the generator or its sources:

    npm run make-packs
